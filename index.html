01

Property Valuation

Understand Your Property's True Worth

Traditional property valuations often miss critical factors that can significantly impact your land's value. Our geospatial analysis goes beyond standard assessments to uncover hidden opportunities.

  • Energy Infrastructure Proximity: Distance to power lines, substations, and transmission corridors can dramatically increase property value for energy development
  • Solar & Renewable Potential: Comprehensive analysis of solar irradiance, wind patterns, and renewable energy viability
  • Development Suitability: Assessment of zoning, environmental constraints, and infrastructure access
  • Market Intelligence: Real-time data on energy markets, land transactions, and investment trends

Property valuations that account for energy infrastructure can reveal 20-40% more value than traditional assessments.

02

Energy Infrastructure Investment

Maximize Your Land's Income Potential

Energy infrastructure development represents one of the fastest-growing sectors in real estate. Property owners can generate substantial long-term income through strategic partnerships.

  • Utility-Scale Solar: Lease your land for large solar arrays, generating 20-30 years of stable income with minimal disruption
  • Data Center Development: High-value developments with proximity to power infrastructure and fiber networks
  • Battery Storage: Critical grid infrastructure that provides reliable lease income and supports renewable energy
  • Wind Energy: Long-term lease agreements with wind farm developers for properties with suitable wind resources

Land leases for utility-scale solar typically generate $500-$1,500 per acre annually for 25+ years.

03

Building & System Upgrades

Reduce Costs & Environmental Impact

Building energy upgrades deliver immediate cost savings while increasing property value and reducing your environmental footprint. Our partners provide turnkey solutions with zero upfront investment options.

  • Solar Installation: Residential and commercial solar systems that can eliminate 70-100% of electricity costs
  • HVAC Optimization: Smart building systems that reduce heating and cooling costs by 30-50%
  • LED Retrofits: Lighting upgrades that pay for themselves in 1-3 years while improving workspace quality
  • Energy Procurement: Competitive bidding for electricity and gas contracts in deregulated markets

Commercial buildings can reduce energy costs by 40-60% through comprehensive efficiency upgrades.

About Snapshot

Bridging property owners with the energy transition through advanced geospatial technology and expert partnerships.

Our Mission

Snapshot was created to help property owners navigate the complex landscape of energy infrastructure opportunities. We believe every property owner deserves access to the same data-driven insights and expert connections that major developers use.

Our platform combines advanced geospatial analysis with a curated network of industry-leading partners to deliver personalized recommendations that maximize property value and support the clean energy transition.

Our Technology

Snapshot utilizes cutting-edge geospatial analysis tools to evaluate properties across multiple dimensions:

  • Solar Potential: Global Horizontal Irradiance (GHI) analysis to determine solar energy viability
  • Infrastructure Proximity: Distance to electrical grids, transmission lines, and substations
  • Environmental Assessment: Wetlands, floodplains, protected areas, and regulatory constraints
  • Market Analysis: Energy market conditions and deregulation status
  • Development Suitability: Property characteristics for various energy and infrastructure projects
